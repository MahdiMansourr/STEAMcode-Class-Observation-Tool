Observation criteria

Remarks may be entered in any language and remain intact when the interface language changes.

1

Academic Proficiency

1.Demonstrates knowledge of the subject matter/content
2.Abides by plan & annual distribution
3.Possesses language proficiency
4.Exhibits clear enunciation, accurate pronunciation, and effective voice projection
5.Uses appropriate and relevant materials and resources
6.Uses varied active learning strategies
7.Gives on-going constructive feedback and motivation to all learners / provides assistance when necessary / praises each student’s active involvement
8.Makes clear transitions from one activity to another
9.Provides closure (wrap-up) to the lesson
2

Classroom Management (safety & discipline)

10.Grabs learners’ attention
11.Monitors the classroom effectively (rotates in class / keeps eye contact / follows school safety policies and procedures)
12.Applies alternative forms of corrective action to classroom behavior
13.Manages interaction effectively and positively (student-teacher / student-student)
14.Manages time to meet proper intervals for pre-, during, and post-activities of the lesson
3

Professional Disposition

15.Demonstrates enthusiasm and warmth
16.Makes use of emerging teachable moments
17.Possesses computer literacy skills (active board - laptop - visuals…)
